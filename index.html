How do I book a taxi in Calicut?+

Call +91 98957 03350 or +91 9656 299 679, or fill in the trip form to prepare a WhatsApp enquiry. Our team will confirm availability, the fare and pickup details before your booking is final.

Can I book a Kozhikode to Wayanad package?+

Yes. Enquire about a day trip or a longer Wayanad getaway with cab travel, sightseeing and stay assistance. We will help tailor the itinerary to your dates, interests and group size.

Is accommodation included in a Wayanad package?+

We offer stay assistance. Accommodation, meals, sightseeing entry fees and any other inclusions will be discussed and confirmed in your individual quote. They are not automatically included in a cab enquiry.

Do you provide airport transfers and group transport?+

We take enquiries for Calicut Airport pickups and drop-offs, travellers and tourist buses, and call-driver services. Contact us with your travel time and group size to check availability.
